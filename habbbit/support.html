Support center

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about Habbbit. Can't find what you need? Contact us.

How do I create a new habit?

Tap the plus button on the main habits screen, give it a name, choose a color and icon, and set your frequency. It's added to your list instantly.

What is the pixel streak grid?

Every habit has its own mosaic of tiles — one lights up each time you check in. Over time the grid fills into a piece of art that reflects your consistency.

How does the focus timer work?

Start a session from any habit to track live time. You can pause, resume, or stop it at any point, and completed sessions feed directly into your analytics.

Can I see when I'm most productive?

Yes. The Analytics tab breaks down your activity by hour and by day over 7, 30, or 365-day windows, so you can spot your best patterns.

How do I archive or delete a habit?

Open the habit's menu (the three dots) and choose Archive or Delete. Archived habits and their history stay accessible under Settings → Archived Habits.

Can I share my progress?

Yes — tap the share icon on any analytics screen to generate a card with your streak art and stats, ready to post or send.

Is my data backed up?

You can export and manage your data from Settings → Data Management. See our Privacy Policy for details on what's stored and where.

I found a bug — what do I do?

Please contact us with your device model, OS version, and steps to reproduce it — that helps us fix things fast.

Still stuck?

Our team is happy to help with anything the FAQ didn't cover.
